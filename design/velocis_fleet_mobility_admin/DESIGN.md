## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a high-precision, executive-tier control plane for enterprise vehicle fleet orchestration, automated booking pipelines, and operational telematics. Balancing administrative utility with modern mobility sophistication, the aesthetic marries **Modern Corporate** clarity with subtle **Tactile Precision**.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Executive Command:** Deep violet establishes architectural gravity and institutional trust, signaling operational authority over multi-asset portfolios.
- **Dynamic Velocity:** Violet-purple and soft gold accents inject momentum, precision, and VIP hospitality without descending into consumer flashiness.
- **Operational Calm:** Light stone neutrals and surgical white surfaces reduce visual fatigue during prolonged dispatch and telemetry monitoring sessions.

### Design Aesthetics
- **Engineered Restraint:** Strict spatial hierarchy, crisp delineation, and 1px architectural borders prioritize rapid data parsing.
- **Luminescent Highlights:** Strategic, focused touches of warm gold and bright status indicators prevent dense data tables and telemetry matrices from feeling sterile.

## Colors

The palette leverages a calibrated balance of deep chromatic authority, refined support violets, tactile light neutrals, and deterministic semantic signals.

### Core Architecture
- **Primary (`#3E0F8D` - Deep Imperial Violet):** Anchors primary calls-to-action, persistent navigation bars, primary stat callouts, and key interaction focal points.
- **Secondary (`#9564DD` - Vibrant Iris Violet):** Governs active calendar states, range selections, telemetry trendlines, and secondary buttons.
- **Tertiary / Accent (`#E4DA72` - Soft Gold Yellow):** Reserved for VIP customer badges, priority reservation tags, high-value asset flags, and focal telemetry highlights.
- **Neutral & Surfaces:**
  - **Canvas Base (`#EEEEEE`):** Ground level providing high-contrast separation for floating modular widgets.
  - **Card Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** High-opacity pure white for focused information density.
  - **Subtle Surface (`#F8F9FA`):** Column zebra striping, table headers, and sunken input wells.

### Semantic Telematics & Fleet Status
- **Available (`#10B981` Emerald):** Vehicle ready for dispatch, battery fully charged, inspection cleared.
- **Rented / Active (`#6366F1` Indigo / Secondary Purple):** Active booking in progress, vehicle in transit.
- **Maintenance / Pending (`#F59E0B` Amber):** Scheduled servicing, inspection required, deposit pending.
- **Canceled / Overdue / Warning (`#F43F5E` Rose):** Contract breach, late return, low fuel/battery alert, engine fault.

### Typography Hierarchy
- **Text Primary (`#1E1B4B` Midnight Slate):** High-contrast headline and primary metric legibility.
- **Text Secondary (`#475569` Cool Slate):** Meta-labels, tabular descriptions, and unit captions.
- **Text Muted (`#94A3B8` Slate Fog):** Disabled states, gridlines, and placeholder iconography.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural headers and numerical milestones with **Inter** for dense dashboard arrays and operational telemetry.

### Pairing Mechanics
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Headings & Metric Numbers):** Delivers clean geometry with friendly, modern legibility. Used for KPI figures, vehicle plate titles, and main modal headers.
- **Inter (Body, Tables, Meta Data):** Provides utilitarian neutral readability with tabular figures (`tnum`) enabled across all table columns, odometer readings, and revenue breakdowns to guarantee vertical decimal alignment.

### Numerical and Telemetry Treatments
- Always use tabular number variants (`font-feature-settings: 'tnum' on, 'zero' on`) for vehicle telemetry (fuel %, speed km/h, daily rates, GPS coordinates).
- Data labels must preserve a subtle uppercase tracking style when below 12px to maintain instant readability under rapid glance conditions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed around an **8pt modular baseline** and a **12-column adaptive fluid grid** configured for multi-tier density.

### Breakpoints & Viewport Grid
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12 columns, 24px (`space-lg`) gutters, 32px (`space-xl`) outer page margins. Supports side-by-side telemetry maps, Gantt availability tracks, and deep fleet lists.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns, 16px (`space-md`) gutters, 24px (`space-lg`) margins. Collapsible side-navigation collapses to an icon rail (64px wide); stat metrics flow into a 2x2 matrix.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 12px (`space-sm`) gutters, 16px (`space-md`) margins. Full-width modular cards, horizontal scrolling for calendar pills, and sliding bottom sheets for car inspections.

### Layout Containers
- **Master Admin Shell:** Fixed left rail (260px desktop default), fluid main content area restricted to a maximum container width of `1680px` for consistent ergonomic scanning.
- **Widget Spacing:** Interior card padding standardizes to `20px` or `24px` (`space-lg`), with tight internal groupings spaced at `8px` (`space-xs`) or `12px` (`space-sm`).

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve administrative clarity and speed, this design system avoids heavy shadows, instead employing **Tonal Layering with Violet-Tinted Ambient Diffusions**.

### Depth Layers
- **Level 0 (Floor Canvas):** Neutral Light Grey (`#EEEEEE`). Non-interactive backdrop.
- **Level 1 (Structural Cards & Surfaces):** Crisp Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border (`rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.08)`).
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Interactive Popovers, Hover Cards):** White background paired with an ambient shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(62, 15, 141, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Inspection Slide-overs):** `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(62, 15, 141, 0.16), 0 1px 3px 0px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`. Accompanied by a 30% deep violet backdrop tint (`rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.45)`) with a 4px blur.

### Depth Rules
- Never use non-tinted generic grey drop shadows. All elevation shadows incorporate a baseline primary violet hue (`rgba(62, 15, 141, X)`) to keep surfaces cohesive.
- Borderlines on dark surfaces (such as navigation or telemetry overlays) utilize `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system implements a **Level 2 (Rounded)** posture, giving components approachable geometry balanced with the crisp edges required for industrial fleet administration.

### Curvature Tokens
- **Micro Elements (Badges, Checkboxes, Micro Chips):** `6px` radius (`rounded-sm`).
- **Input Fields, Table Cells, and Action Buttons:** `8px` radius (`rounded-md` / standard).
- **Cards, Panels, and Fleet Modules:** `16px` radius (`rounded-lg`).
- **Telemetry Indicators, Calendar Pills, and Quick Filters:** Full continuous curves (`9999px` / capsule shape).

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary:** Deep Violet (`#3E0F8D`) background, white typography, subtle top highlight border (`rgba(255,255,255,0.15)` inset). On hover, tint with `#4C14A8` and shift elevation.
- **Secondary:** Transparent fill with a 1.5px border in `#9564DD` and text in `#3E0F8D`.
- **Accent / Special Action:** Soft Gold Yellow (`#E4DA72`) background with Midnight Slate text (`#1E1B4B`) for priority upgrades and VIP booking overrides.
- **Destructive:** Soft Rose base (`#FFE4E6`) with `#F43F5E` label for termination flows; shifts to solid crimson on confirmation.

### Data Tables (Fleet Inventory & Booking Ledgers)
- **Header:** Background `#F8F9FA`, bottom border 1px solid `rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.08)`. Labels in `Inter 11px Bold`, all-caps, with slate tinting.
- **Row Architecture:** Minimum height `56px`. Alternating soft hover state `#F3F0FA` (5% tint of primary).
- **Cell Alignment:** String metrics align left; numeric rates, odometer values, and vehicle statuses align right or center with tabular numerals.

### Stat Cards (Fleet Utilization & Revenue)
- **Structure:** Pure white card with 16px radius and light outer stroke.
- **Composition:** Top row features an icon capsule tinted with the metric’s semantic color (e.g., emerald for utilization rate), accompanied by a trend badge (+4.8% vs last week). Center showcases a bold 28px Jakarta Sans KPI figure, followed by an Inter 12px descriptor.

### Telemetry Badges & Status Chips
- **Pill Badges:** 24px height, full-radius capsule.
- **Available:** Light emerald background (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`), solid emerald text (`#059669`), leading 6px glowing dot indicator.
- **Rented:** Soft indigo background (`rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.12)`), text (`#4F46E5`).
- **Maintenance:** Soft amber background (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.14)`), text (`#D97706`).
- **Telemetry Readouts:** Battery charge and fuel levels use horizontal capsule bars with real-time numeric readouts (`Inter Data-Mono`).

### Interactive Calendar & Dispatch Timeline Pills
- **Timeline Grid:** Horizontal axis partitioned into calendar days.
- **Reservation Pills:** Rounded capsules spanning timeline hours/days. Background matches vehicle status (Deep Violet for confirmed, Iris for in-progress). Displays compact renter initials, plate ID, and turnaround buffer indicators.

### Form Inputs & Filters
- **Input Fields:** Height 40px, 1px border `rgba(71, 85, 105, 0.25)`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#1E1B4B`.
- **Focus State:** 2px ring in Violet Purple (`#9564DD`) with subtle offset.
